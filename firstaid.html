🐾 Before You Begin

Stay calm and observe the animal from a safe distance. Injured or frightened animals may bite or scratch. Avoid handling an unfamiliar animal unnecessarily.

Important: This tool provides preliminary guidance only. It cannot diagnose an animal or replace a veterinarian. For severe injuries, breathing difficulties, heavy bleeding, suspected poisoning, or unconsciousness, contact a veterinarian or animal rescue service immediately.

FIRST AID ASSISTANT

What is happening to the animal?

Describe the animal's condition and any symptoms you have observed.

💚 What Should You Do?

  • Keep yourself and the animal away from traffic or other immediate dangers, if safe to do so.
  • Contact a qualified veterinarian or animal rescue organisation for professional help.
  • Do not give human medicines or attempt treatments unless a veterinarian instructs you to.
  • Do not force food or water on an injured or unconscious animal.

Need More Help?

If an animal needs urgent assistance, use our emergency reporting page. If you are looking for veterinary care or NGO assistance, visit our care-finding page.
